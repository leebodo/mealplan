    Rules this follows

    Tuesday never cooks.If Monday dinner is out, you're asked whether Monday cooking still happens; if not, Recipe A really does move to Sunday night and its window shortens to Mon–Wed.
    Hosting never rewrites your week.If a hosting date can't safely use the ordinary batch, it gets its own extra batch on that day. Your normal dinners stay exactly where they are.
    Event order doesn't matter.Dates are resolved together, so entering the same two meals in either order gives the same plan.
    Batches stay even, guests included.Whole cans and whole tofu blocks. Four for you plus three guests becomes eight, with the spare assigned rather than left unexplained.
    Nothing goes to work.Weekday lunches stay packaged. A spare serving joins another home sitting, two servings maximum.
    Batches are always even.0, 2, 4 or up — never an odd number, so never a half can or part block. Hosting rounds up and the extra portion goes round for seconds.
    Meals out cover what disappears.A two-serving sitting genuinely goes, and that meal is sized to replace it. One estimate is used throughout — no competing numbers for the same meal.
    Planned personal intake stays under ~2,800 kcal a day.Counting anything eaten out. A spare hosting portion isn't counted — it's for guests. A heavy day gives its Huel back rather than piling on.
    Crema is always made on Saturday.It covers this weekend's tacos plus a standard four Burrito Bowls next Monday. Even in a week with no tacos at all, ¾ cup still gets made — nothing else produces it, and Monday depends on it.
    Drinks aren't part of a meal out.Soylent, Evolve, Huel and coffee stay on the day. Only the meal is replaced.
    Daily sodium is shown, not policed.Only the weekly average is a limit, and the restaurant assumption is yours to set.

    Team lunch is costed as the customized Shroomami with bread (645 kcal, 23 g, ~1,780 mg); swap in real figures if you order something else. Nutrition values are otherwise the verified v5 ledger. Meals eaten out are not counted in weekly totals — the planner shows what your own food supplies and how much the outside meal needs to carry. Hosting doesn't change what you eat, only what you cook and buy.
